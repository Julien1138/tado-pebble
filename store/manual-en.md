# Tado — store listing (English)

## Short text

See and control your Tado heating from your wrist.

## Description

**Tado** shows the temperature and heating power of every room in your Tado home, and lets you control it from your watch.

- Room list, room detail, history of the last twelve hours
- Boost a temperature for a chosen time, go back to the schedule, turn a room off
- Presence (Home, Away, Auto) and whole-house shortcuts
- English and French, °C or °F according to your account, optional touchscreen

You need a Tado account with a Tado heating system. Independent app, not affiliated with Tado.

## How to use

1. At first launch, enter the code shown on `login.tado.com/oauth2/device`.
2. **Up / Down**: choose. **Select**: open. **Long press on Select** from the list: menu (history, presence, shortcuts). **Back**: go back.
3. A row's color follows the temperature; the heat lines show the heating power.
